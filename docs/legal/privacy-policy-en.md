# Privacy Policy — “Eco health”

**Effective date:** 6 July 2026
**Last updated:** 6 July 2026

> ⚠️ Host this document at a permanent public URL and reference that URL in Google Play
> Console and App Store Connect.

This Privacy Policy explains what data the **“Eco health”** mobile application (the “App”)
processes, for what purpose and on what basis, and what rights users have.

Operator (rights holder) of the App: **Ekologiya va iqlim oʻzgarishi milliy qoʻmitasi**
(National Committee on Ecology and Climate Change). Developer: **AI laboratoriya** (“we”).

By using the App you agree to this Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

---

## 1. Summary

- The App helps you log nutrition, water, steps and body metrics, and get reference
  nutrition advice generated by artificial intelligence (AI).
- **No account is required.** We do not ask for a login, password, phone number or e-mail.
- **Most data is stored locally on your device** and never leaves it.
- Data is sent to external services **only when you request AI advice**.
- We **show no ads** and **do not sell** your data.

---

## 2. Data we process

### 2.1. Data you enter
- Profile: name (optional), sex, date of birth (age), height, weight, activity level,
  goal (lose / maintain / gain weight), profile photo (optional).
- Diary: logged foods and dishes, water intake, body metrics (fat, muscle, etc.).

### 2.2. Data generated by the App
- Step count and estimated calories burned (from the device step sensor).
- Computed values (calorie target, macros, micronutrients, weight history).
- Technical settings (language, theme, notification preferences).

### 2.3. Data we do NOT collect
- We do not collect precise location, contacts, or call/SMS history.
- We use no advertising identifiers and no third-party trackers/analytics.

---

## 3. Where data is stored

All of the above is **stored locally on the user’s device** (in the app’s private storage).
We do not maintain a server-side user account and do not keep a copy of your diary on our
servers.

---

## 4. AI nutrition advice (sharing with third parties)

When you tap to get AI advice, the App builds a **de-identified set** of nutrition and
profile data and sends it for processing over a secure (HTTPS) connection. This is the only
case in which data leaves the device.

**What is sent:** age, sex, height, weight, activity level, goal, daily targets, aggregate
and average values for calories, macros and micronutrients, and the names of foods/dishes
logged over the selected period.

**What is NOT sent:** your name, photo, contact details or precise location.

**Recipients and purpose:**
1. **Google Firebase (Cloud Functions, App Check)** — a Google LLC cloud platform that
   routes the request. Firebase App Check protects the backend function from unauthorized
   access.
2. **DeepSeek AI service** — a third-party large-language-model provider that processes the
   submitted data set and returns the recommendation text.

The data is used **solely to generate the advice** in response to your request and is not
used by us for advertising or profiling. Processing by these providers is governed by their
own terms and privacy policies.

AI features require an internet connection.

---

## 5. Device permissions

The App may request the following permissions. All are **optional** — core features work
without them, and you can revoke them in device settings at any time.

| Permission | Why it is used |
|---|---|
| Notifications (`POST_NOTIFICATIONS`) | Local reminders for meals, water, the day summary and weigh-ins. Computed on device. |
| Physical activity recognition (`ACTIVITY_RECOGNITION`) | Step counting and estimated calories burned via the device sensor. |
| Exact alarms (`SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM`) | Timely delivery of reminders (falls back to inexact reminders if not granted). |
| Boot completed (`RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED`) | Restore the step counter and reminder schedule after a reboot. |
| Internet (`INTERNET`) | Only to request AI advice. |

Step and notification data **never leaves the device**.

---

## 6. Legal bases

- **Consent** — given on the consent screen at first launch (after entering profile data)
  and when requesting AI advice.
- **Performing functions you requested** — logging and calculations.

You can withdraw consent by disabling the relevant permissions in device settings and/or
deleting data in the App (see section 7).

---

## 7. Retention, deletion and user rights

- Data is kept on the device until you delete it.
- Delete everything: **Profile → Reset data**.
- Uninstalling the App also erases all local data.
- Because we do not store your data on our servers, access/rectification/erasure is handled
  directly in the App on the device.

---

## 8. Children

The App is not intended for children below the age required by your country’s law for
independent consent to data processing, without the consent of a parent or legal guardian.
We do not knowingly collect such children’s data.

---

## 9. Security

Data for AI advice is transmitted over a secure (HTTPS) connection. Calls to the backend
function are protected by Firebase App Check. However, no method of transmission or storage
is completely secure.

---

## 10. Medical disclaimer

The App is for **informational and reference purposes only** and is **not a medical
device**. AI advice, targets and calculations do not replace professional medical advice and
are not intended to diagnose or treat any condition.
